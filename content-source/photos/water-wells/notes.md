## Before using on the site

Consistent with the existing site copy's stance on impact media
("Not every photo, name, or location belongs online" — see the /impact
page and [[venezuela-earthquake-2026 notes]]):

- Confirm who took these and whether the people/location shown (visible
  shoe brand, hand, general setting) are okay to be public.
- No location, community, or partner name is attached to these files —
  if they're meant to illustrate a specific mission (e.g. Community
  Water & Relief), get that context before publishing so captions don't
  overstate or misattribute the source.
- If cleared for public use, optimized copies belong in `src/assets`
  (build-time optimized via Astro) or `public/`, not this folder —
  `content-source/` is reference-only and isn't built into the site.
