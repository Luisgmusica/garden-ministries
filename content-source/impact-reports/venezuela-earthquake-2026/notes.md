## What this is

A thank-you email from Michael to Pastor Isrrael and friends at Deer Flat
(a partner church), acknowledging a gift given in support of Venezuela

dated September 1, 2026.

## Attribution — read before using anywhere

**All operational figures below belong to Samaritan's Purse, not Garden
Ministries.** The email's phrasing ("our work in Venezuela," "our total
response") describes the on-the-ground operation loosely; the field work,
staff, field hospital, and DART team are Samaritan's Purse's. Garden
Ministries' role here is as a **funding/giving channel** — Deer Flat and
other partner churches gave gifts that Garden routed toward Samaritan's
Purse's Venezuela Relief fund (designation code 013692).

Any future site copy describing this must:
- Credit Samaritan's Purse as the implementing organization.
- Describe Garden Ministries' role accurately (facilitating/directing
  donor gifts to a vetted partner response), not as Garden's own field
  operation.
- This lines up with the existing "Community Water & Relief" mission
  copy on the site, which is already framed as **partner-led**
  ("Partner-led support for water access, food, medicine, and
  humanitarian needs... Sensitive locations protected").

## Key figures (Samaritan's Purse, as of Sept 1, 2026 sitrep)

- 6,000+ fatalities, 16,700+ injuries reported (Venezuela earthquake, overall)
- 6 airlifts, 591,887 lbs of cargo delivered
- 100 local church partners distributing relief supplies
- 2 SeaSpring desalination units deployed in Caraballeda
- 56-bed Emergency Field Hospital in La Guaira (ER, ICU, 2 operating rooms,
  pharmacy, lab, outpatient care)
- 7,483 patients treated / 395 surgeries performed
- 5 local health facilities restored via Clinic Restart Modules
- 212 pastors and spouses trained in trauma-informed ministry
- 19,554 solar lights / 27,194 hygiene kits / 12,527 blankets / 458 family
  tents / 328,281 plates of food / 20,690 dry food kits distributed
- 185,690 gal. of water filtered
- 39,195 households assisted
