Summary

The home page and one mission page now show real, current documentation of Garden's water-well work in place of a generic stock photograph. Two other mission pages were deliberately left text-only rather than filled with AI-generated stand-in photos of unrelated people. A separate batch of field photos from a partner's disaster response was reviewed and withheld for reasons detailed below.

The guiding rule throughout was the one already stated on this site's own Impact page: not every photo, name, or location belongs online. Every decision below — what went up, what stayed down, and what's proposed next — was made against that standard rather than against how complete a page looked.

What changed, and why

Four editorial decisions, each recorded with its rationale.

Done

Homepage hero: stock Idaho landscape photo → rotating set of 4 real well-project photos

The previous hero image was a generic stock photograph with no connection to Garden's actual work. It's replaced by four real images from a documented well-construction project — two photographs and two frames drawn from project video — cross-fading every four seconds. No individual is identifiable in any of the four.

Done

Community Water & Relief mission page: added one real photo and one real video

This is the one mission with enough safe, real material on hand — a photo of the well-ring installation and a short video looking down a finished well. Both were re-encoded and checked for playback before publishing; neither shows an identifiable face or a placename.

Deferred by design

Local Family Care & Ministry Strengthening: kept text-only

Two AI-generated stock images were prepared as placeholders (volunteers serving a meal; a community garden). They were not used. Standing in fake photos of unconnected people to fill an empty slot works against the same authenticity this update is meant to build — better to show nothing until the ministry teams have their own photos to share.

Withheld

~40 field photos and video from a Venezuela relief distribution: not published

This batch shows identifiable minors receiving aid, with no consent process on record, and includes a visible police-station sign that places the exact location. It also documents work led by a partner organization (see below), not a Garden-run program. None of it was used anywhere on the site.

Suggestions: telling the Venezuela earthquake response story

Separate from the withheld field photos, Garden holds a legitimate, tellable story here: a gift from Deer Flat, routed by Garden, helped fund Samaritan's Purse's emergency response to the Venezuela earthquake. That story can be told well without the photos above. Four options, in suggested order:

1

Link to Samaritan's Purse's own report and video, rather than re-hosting field photos

Samaritan's Purse already publishes a public situation report and a produced video of this response. Pointing to their materials tells the story with imagery that already cleared their own consent and safety review — Garden doesn't need to (and shouldn't) republish the sensitive photos it received privately.

Recommended starting point
2

A short "how your gift traveled" module, using aggregate figures only

A brief narrative — Garden and Deer Flat directed a gift toward Samaritan's Purse's response; here's the scale of what that response reached — sourced from Samaritan's Purse's public sitrep, credited by name, with no names or images of individual recipients.

Longer-term, once 1–2 are live

What would help most next

Concrete items that would materially improve the site, beyond what's already decided above.

Consent process

A standard photo/video release form for field visits

The Venezuela batch was withheld largely because there's no consent on record for the identifiable minors in it. A simple release process for future trips — used going forward by Garden's own team and asked of partners — means new material arrives already clear to publish, instead of reviewed and set aside after the fact.

Real photos

Current, consented photos or video for Local Family Care and Ministry Strengthening

These are the two mission pages still text-only. Even a handful of real images — a distribution day, a training session, a pastor's home church — would let both pages match Community Water's treatment.

Captions & names

Sign-off and captions for the leadership family photo already on file

A posed family portrait is on hand and reads as appropriate for an About page, but it needs the board's confirmation that it's cleared for public use, plus names and roles to caption it correctly.

Verified numbers

Board-approved figures for the Impact page, per mission

The Impact page currently withholds public numbers pending "leadership verification." Approved figures — families served, wells completed, funds distributed — would let that page speak in specifics instead of describing its own review process.

Org credentials

501(c)(3)/EIN and financial-transparency documents

The site asks for trust ("A gift should not disappear after checkout") but doesn't yet show the standard donor trust signals — registered nonprofit status, a link to Form 990 or audited financials. Worth adding once donation processing itself is connected.

Partnership terms

A clear description of the Samaritan's Purse relationship

To word the Venezuela story accurately (suggestion above), it helps to know exactly how Garden's role should be described — a one-time designated gift, an ongoing referral relationship, or something more formal — rather than assuming.

Source media

Original, higher-resolution photos or video from the well project

Two of the four hero images are still frames pulled from phone video, not native photographs — usable, but a notch below the two real photos in sharpness at large sizes. Original stills from that same visit, if any exist, would sharpen the hero further.

For the board

  • Confirm the Venezuela field photos stay unpublished, and that only Samaritan's Purse's own public materials are used if the earthquake story goes on the site.
  • Choose which of the four suggestions above to authorize, and in what order.
  • Confirm Deer Flat is comfortable being named publicly before any recognition goes live.
  • Decide whether to commission real photos for Local Family Care and Ministry Strengthening, or leave those pages text-only for now.
  • Approve deployment of the completed hero-carousel and Community Water updates to the live site.
